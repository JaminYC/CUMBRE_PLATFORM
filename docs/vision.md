# Vision

## Purpose

Define CUMBRE_PLATFORM as an adaptive learning ecosystem for students, teachers, and administrators.

## Product Intent

- Personalize learning paths based on performance, behavior, and context
- Give teachers visibility into progress, risks, and recommended interventions
- Provide administrators with operational and academic analytics
- Use AI to improve tutoring, retrieval, assessment, and recommendation flows

## Success Signals

- Better learner engagement and retention
- Faster teacher feedback cycles
- Measurable academic improvement
- Reliable multi-role platform operations
